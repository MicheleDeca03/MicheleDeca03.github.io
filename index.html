    Machine learning for the prioritization of Dyson sphere candidates
    P. Mignone, M. De Carolis
    Expert Systems with Applications, 131232, 2026.
    DOI: 10.1016/j.eswa.2026.131232
    PDF (Open Access)
    Dyson spheres are hypothetical megastructures proposed to partially or fully enclose a star and reprocess its radiation, producing mid-infrared excesses that may appear as rare anomalies in large stellar catalogues. No confirmed Dyson sphere is known, but the systematic prioritization of stars with unexplained photometric behaviour offers a practical way to support targeted astrophysical follow-up. In this work, we present a machine learning framework for the prioritization of potential Dyson sphere candidates, combining a two-phase design with normal and anomalous star models. Isolation Forest delivers the most accurate and stable overall ranking. The pipeline recovers known excess stars and highlights new candidates for follow-up.
